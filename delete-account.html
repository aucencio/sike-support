Delete Your SIKE Account

You can permanently delete your SIKE account and the information SIKE controls from inside the SIKE app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open SIKE and go to your Profile.
  2. Choose Delete Account.
  3. Confirm Delete Permanently.

If you cannot access the app, email SIKE Support from the email address on your account.

What deletion removes

Deleting your account removes your SIKE profile, tasks, reminders, schedules, grocery-list entries, family memberships and invitations, saved COSMO memories, and device notification records that SIKE controls.

What may be retained

We may retain limited information only as needed for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, completed transactions, or to enforce agreements. Purchases and subscription records are managed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own retention practices.

For details, see the SIKE Privacy Policy.
