SIKE Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

SIKE: I Got You (“SIKE,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information SIKE may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, SIKE may process:

  • Account or user identifiers
  • Tasks and reminders
  • Grocery-list and checklist information
  • Family-sharing information
  • App settings and preferences
  • Notification tokens and notification settings
  • Location information when location features are enabled
  • Subscription and entitlement status
  • Device and app information
  • AI prompts or requests submitted through AI-powered features
  • Diagnostic information needed to maintain and improve the app

2. Location Information

SIKE may request access to your device location for features such as:

  • Smart Places
  • Arrival reminders
  • Departure or leaving alerts
  • Location-aware tasks
  • Nearby-context reminders

Location access is only used when required for enabled features and is subject to the permissions you grant through your device.

You may change location permissions at any time through your device settings.

3. Family Sharing

If you use SIKE Family features, information such as shared grocery items, reminders, or checklists may be visible to other members of your SIKE family group.

Only share information you are comfortable making available to those members.

4. Notifications

SIKE may use notification tokens and related information to deliver reminders, tasks, location alerts, family updates, and other enabled notifications.

Notification permissions may be changed through your device settings.

5. AI Features

When you use AI-powered features, information you submit may be transmitted to AI service providers to generate responses.

Do not submit information you do not want processed by an AI provider.

SIKE may use services provided by OpenAI or other AI infrastructure providers to deliver AI functionality.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions may be processed through:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play
  • RevenueCat

SIKE does not directly receive or store complete payment-card information used for App Store or Google Play purchases.

We may receive subscription information such as plan type, entitlement status, renewal status, and purchase identifiers.

7. Cloud and Authentication Services

SIKE may use Supabase for cloud infrastructure, authentication, databases, synchronization, and related backend services.

Information necessary to provide SIKE functionality may be stored or processed through these services.

8. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide SIKE functionality
  • Save and synchronize user data
  • Deliver reminders and notifications
  • Provide Smart Places and location features
  • Support family-sharing functionality
  • Manage subscription access
  • Generate AI-powered responses
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Prevent abuse or misuse
  • Improve SIKE’s reliability and features
  • Comply with legal obligations

9. Information Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate SIKE, including providers for:

  • Cloud infrastructure
  • Authentication
  • AI functionality
  • Subscription management
  • App-store payments
  • Notifications
  • Technical monitoring

We may also disclose information when legally required.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide SIKE, maintain user accounts, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce agreements.

When an account is deleted, associated information will be deleted or de-identified where reasonably possible, subject to legitimate legal or technical retention requirements.

11. Account and Data Deletion

Where SIKE supports user accounts, users may request account deletion through available in-app functionality or by contacting SIKE support.

Deletion requests may require identity or account verification.

12. Children

SIKE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
