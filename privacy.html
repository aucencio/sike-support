SIKE Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

SIKE: I Got You ("SIKE," "we," or "us") helps you manage tasks, reminders, schedules, grocery lists, Smart Places alerts, family sharing, and COSMO voice features. This policy explains what SIKE uses and why.

Information SIKE uses

  • Account information: the email address and optional name you provide when you create or recover an account.
  • Your content: tasks, reminders, schedules, grocery-list entries, family-sharing information, and COSMO messages or memories you choose to save.
  • Location: precise location only when you turn on location features. SIKE uses it for departure alerts and Smart Places reminders. You can turn location access off in SIKE or your device settings.
  • Microphone and speech: when you choose voice input, your device's speech-recognition service turns your request into text. SIKE does not keep recordings of your voice.
  • Device and notification information: a device installation identifier and notification token so SIKE can deliver reminders you enable.
  • Diagnostics: limited technical event and error information used to keep SIKE reliable, such as whether a notification was scheduled or a feature failed.

How SIKE uses information

SIKE uses your information to provide the features you request, keep your lists and account available after you reopen the app, deliver reminders and location-based alerts, coordinate an invited family group, protect against misuse, support the app, and improve reliability. SIKE does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Service providers

SIKE uses trusted service providers to operate the app: Supabase for account and app data; OpenAI for COSMO responses and hosted voice when you use those features; RevenueCat and Apple or Google for subscription status and purchases; Expo for notification delivery; and Google Maps Platform for optional nearby-place recognition. These providers process information only to provide their services to SIKE.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. SIKE does not receive or store your payment-card number.

Choices and deletion

You can disable notifications, location, or microphone access in your device settings. You can delete your SIKE account from the app. Account deletion removes the account data SIKE controls, subject to limited records retained where required for security, legal obligations, or completed transactions.

Children

SIKE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when SIKE changes. For privacy questions, contact aucenciom.am@gmail.com.
